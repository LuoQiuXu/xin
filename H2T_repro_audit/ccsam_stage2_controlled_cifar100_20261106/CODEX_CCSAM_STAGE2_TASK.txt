FORMAL DESIGN REFERENCE
C0_BALANCED_CE:
Frozen-backbone class-aware balanced sampling + unweighted CE. This is a matched cRT-style control that retains the Clean Stage I classifier initialization; do not call it strict canonical cRT with a randomly reinitialized head.

C1_CCSAM_FEATURE_GRAD:
CC-SAM Stage II-inspired class-conditional gradient-ascent perturbation in the 64-D GAP embedding, with class weights and alpha schedule.

C2_WEIGHTED_CE_NO_PERTURB:
Same sampler, class weights, alpha schedule, optimizer, and initialization as C1, but delta is exactly zero.

Primary comparison: C1 minus C2.
Secondary comparisons: C2 minus C0, and C1 minus C0.

All three Stage-II seeds share one fixed Clean Stage I backbone. Do not describe them as three independent Stage I runs or as cross-dataset generalization.
