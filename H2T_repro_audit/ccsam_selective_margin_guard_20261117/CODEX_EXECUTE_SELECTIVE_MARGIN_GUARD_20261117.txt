SCIENTIFIC SCOPE
- D0: original weighted CE, no feature perturbation.
- D1: original full CC-SAM class-conditional feature-gradient perturbation.
- D2: D1 plus Many-input-only full-distribution KL preservation.
- D3: unchanged D1 objective plus the one fixed Selective Many-to-Tail Margin Guard (SMG).

SMG fixed settings:
- Many threshold: official TRAIN count > 100.
- Non-Many outputs: official TRAIN count <= 100.
- Teacher: matching same-dataset/same-seed D0 epoch-30 classifier, frozen.
- Eligible input: Many true label and positive D0 teacher Many-to-non-Many margin > 1e-6.
- Retained margin fraction: 0.5.
- Epsilon: 1e-6.
- Guard coefficient: 1.0.
- Teacher/student logits are evaluated on clean cached features.
- D3 = unchanged D1 loss + 1.0 * SMG loss.
- No D2 KL term is used in D3.
