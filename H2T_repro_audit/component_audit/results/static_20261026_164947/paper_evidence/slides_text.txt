Method
• Two-stage training scheme (Kang et al. 2020) .
• The second stage involves dual-branch sampling: (1) fused branch -- class-balanced data ;
(2) fusing branch -- instance-wise data.
• Fuse the features of the two branches. Only use the labels of the fused branch as ground
truth.
Kang, B.; Xie, S.; Rohrbach, M.; Yan, Z.; Gordo, A.; Feng, J.; and Kalantidis, Y. 2020. Decoupling representation and classifier for long-tailed recognition. In ICLR.

Conclusion
Pros:
Simple but effective:
• A plug-and-play module;
• H2T produces abundant features to augment tail classes;
• H2T generates two opposing forces that restrain each other, preventing
excessive sacrifices in head class accuracy.
Cons:
Sacrifice head class performance:
• H2T  only adjusts the decision boundary without altering the feature
distribution.
