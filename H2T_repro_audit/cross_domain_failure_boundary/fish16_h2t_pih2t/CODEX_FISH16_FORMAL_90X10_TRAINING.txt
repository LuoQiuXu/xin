5. AMP AND OPTIMIZER-STEP ACCOUNTING
======================================================================

Reuse amp_recovery.py and its fail-closed behavior. Do not replace it.

The only recoverable non-finite gradient event is exactly one positive or
negative Inf at:
  extractor.pif.fuse.weight[0,1,0]

The event must:
  - contain no NaN;
  - contain exactly one Inf in the entire optimizer parameter set;
  - occur only in B0 Stage-I;
  - be handled by native enabled GradScaler;
  - cause a real skipped optimizer update and a scale backoff;
  - be followed by a successful real optimizer update at the reduced scale.

Never clamp, clip, zero, replace, or manually discard gradients to bypass the
gate. Missing gradients, NaNs, any other Inf, unverified scaler skips, or
non-finite forward values are fatal.

Count actual optimizer.step calls with OptimizerStepCounter. Do not infer
updates from attempted windows. Record, per epoch and per condition:
  attempted optimizer windows, actual optimizer updates, AMP skips, scaler
  scale transitions, consecutive skips, samples processed, and gradient audits.

The previous B0 one-epoch smoke had two native skips and 1,015 actual updates
from 1,017 attempted windows. Preserve this fact in the historical audit. Do
not compensate for those skips and do not require later epochs to reproduce
the same skip count or batch positions. Apply the same narrow event-level gate
to future B0 Stage-I epochs and fail closed on anything else.

A0/A1/A2/B1/B2 must not silently skip optimizer updates. Stage-II extractor
parameters and BN buffers must remain unchanged.

If a fatal numerical event or CUDA OOM occurs, preserve the partial run,
write FAILURE.json and stop. Do not automatically lower LR, change precision,
change batch size, change accumulation, or retry with altered settings.
