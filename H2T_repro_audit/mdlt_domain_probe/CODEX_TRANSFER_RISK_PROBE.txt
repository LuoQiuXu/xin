SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
Can a directional feature-compatibility score predict whether a proposed channel substitution increases the receiver's cross-entropy, better than simple perturbation magnitude and domain mismatch?

The script compares:
1. Actual outcome: delta_CE = CE(fused receiver) - CE(original receiver); positive means harmful under the fixed H2T-Global classifier.
2. Directional score: first-order Taylor estimate grad_f CE(original) dot masked(donor - receiver).
3. Baselines: masked relative L2, masked cosine distance, donor-label mismatch, baseline CE/negative margin, and domain mismatch.
4. A paired CrossDomain-minus-DomainMatched comparison using the same receiver, donor class, and channel mask. Donor image identity necessarily differs between domain conditions.
